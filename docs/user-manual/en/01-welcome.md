# Chapter 1: Welcome to OmniPanel-go

## What is OmniPanel-go?

OmniPanel-go turns any touch device on your home network — like a tablet, phone, or old laptop — into a custom control panel for your games. Think of it as building your own cockpit dashboard or mission control center, right on your iPad or Android tablet.

Imagine playing a space sim and having a second screen with buttons for landing gear, sliders for throttle, and a touchpad for camera control. That's what OmniPanel-go does.

## What Makes It Different?

- **No accounts needed** — No sign-ups, no passwords, no cloud services
- **Works offline** — Everything runs on your own computer
- **Completely private** — All data stays on your local network
- **Free and open-source** — No hidden costs or premium features
- **One file to run** — No complicated installation process

## How Does It Work?

Here's the simple version:

1. You run OmniPanel-go on your gaming PC
2. You design control panels using a visual editor (drag and drop)
3. You open the panel on your tablet by visiting a web address
4. When you tap buttons or move sliders on your tablet, OmniPanel-go sends those inputs to your game as if they came from a real game controller

Your game sees OmniPanel-go as a regular joystick or mouse. No special game mods or plugins needed.

## What Do You Need?

### Required

- **A gaming PC** running Windows or Linux (macOS is not supported for game input)
- **A touch device** — any tablet, phone, or laptop with a touchscreen and a web browser
- **Both devices on the same WiFi network** — they need to be connected to the same router

### Optional

- **Multiple touch devices** — you can have different panels on different devices
- **A second monitor** — handy for keeping the OmniPanel-go dashboard visible while gaming

## What Can You Build?

Here are some examples:

- **Flight simulator cockpit** — throttle sliders, flap buttons, gear toggle
- **Racing game dashboard** — gear selector, boost button, brake balance slider
- **Space game control panel** — weapon groups, shield management, navigation
- **General gaming utility** — volume controls, screenshot button, app launcher
