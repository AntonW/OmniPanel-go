## Quick Start

1. **Run OmniPanel-go** on your gaming PC
2. **Open your browser** and go to `http://your-pc-ip:3000`
3. **Create a panel** using the visual Editor
4. **Open the panel** on your tablet or phone
5. **Play your game** with a custom control panel
